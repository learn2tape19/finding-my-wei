# Article I — Purpose

The purpose of this document is to define the permanent institutional architecture of Finding My Wei.

It establishes the constitutional relationship between the Founder, the Operating System, and every Mission Expression. It provides a stable framework through which the institution may grow without losing coherence, purpose, or continuity.

This document defines **what exists**.

It intentionally does not define **how repositories are implemented**. Implementation is governed separately by **FC-002 — Repository Architecture**.

---

# Article II — Mission

The institution exists for one purpose:

> **Helping People Feel Better.**

Every repository, project, publication, course, product, client engagement, and future initiative derives its purpose from this mission.

If a proposed initiative does not advance this mission, it does not belong within the institution.

---

# Article III — Founder

The Founder is the originating source of the institution.

The Founder establishes:

- Mission
- Vision
- Constitutional authority
- Strategic direction
- Final approval

The Founder delegates implementation but does not delegate constitutional authority.

The public professional identity of the Founder is:

**Drew Freedman — The Boston Bodyworker**

The Boston Bodyworker is the enduring professional identity through which the Founder practices, teaches, speaks, invents, and serves.

---

# Article IV — Finding My Wei

Finding My Wei is the Institutional Operating System.

Its purpose is not to create products.

Its purpose is to preserve continuity.

Finding My Wei governs the relationships between the Mission Expressions and provides the constitutional architecture through which work is created, implemented, documented, and preserved.

It exists to ensure that:

- knowledge remains organized,
- decisions remain traceable,
- authority remains clear,
- future stewards understand the institution,
- growth strengthens rather than fragments the mission.

Finding My Wei is infrastructure.

It succeeds when it becomes nearly invisible during normal operation because the Mission Expressions can work with clarity and continuity without repeatedly redesigning their governance.

# Article VII — Repository Boundaries

Each Mission Expression shall maintain one canonical repository when a repository is required for its work.

Repositories are organizational domains, not competing authorities.

Repositories collaborate through shared standards rather than shared constitutional ownership.

No Mission Expression may redefine another Mission Expression's purpose.

No project repository may supersede Finding My Wei's constitutional authority.

Every institutional repository shall declare:

- Purpose
- Canonical scope
- Owner
- Relationship to Finding My Wei
- Dependencies
- Status

Repository implementation details are governed by FC-002.

---

# Article VIII — Canonical Ownership

Every institutional artifact has one canonical home.

Cross-referencing is encouraged.

Uncontrolled duplication is prohibited.

Where multiple copies are operationally necessary, one copy must be explicitly identified as canonical and all derivative copies must reference it.

Examples:

| Artifact | Canonical Home |
|---|---|
| Constitution and institutional governance | Finding My Wei |
| Repository standards | Finding My Wei |
| Digital Stewardship Standard | Finding My Wei |
| Tao Papers | The Tao of Clinical Touch |
| Tao clinical articles | The Tao of Clinical Touch |
| CE courses | Learn2Tape |
| Learn2Tape student materials | Learn2Tape |
| Sidekick Air engineering | StitchCore |
| AeroStitch Core Technology IP | StitchCore |
| Client-specific consulting deliverables | Freedman-Foundry or the approved client repository |

Conflicting canonical sources are not permitted.

# Article IX — Authority

Authority flows downward from mission and Founder authorization.

Experience, evidence, learning, and feedback flow upward.

```text
Mission
  ↓
Founder
  ↓
Finding My Wei
  ↓
Mission Expressions
  ↓
Projects
  ↓
Assets
```

Projects do not redefine Mission Expressions.

Assets do not redefine Projects.

Mission Expressions do not redefine the Constitution.

Finding My Wei does not replace the Founder.

# Article X — Stewardship

Every steward inherits the responsibility to leave the institution clearer than it was found.

Stewardship includes:

- Observation
- Documentation
- Preservation
- Organization
- Verification
- Faithful implementation

Stewardship does not include redefining constitutional purpose without explicit Founder authorization.

Institutional truth resides in version-controlled canonical documents, not in individual memory or AI memory.

- Cross-referencing

# Article XII — Project Placement

Every new initiative shall be placed according to its purpose rather than convenience.

A project belongs to the Mission Expression whose canonical responsibility it advances.

Examples:

- Tao website stewardship → The Tao of Clinical Touch
- Learn2Tape CE course → Learn2Tape
- Sidekick Air manufacturing work → StitchCore
- AREA Salon Studios engagement → Freedman-Foundry

The Boston Bodyworker is the Founder's enduring professional identity and public gateway. It is not a separate constitutional authority over the four Mission Expressions.

# Article XV — Repository Lifecycle

New institutional repositories require Founder approval.

A proposed repository must demonstrate that:

1. It serves a durable institutional purpose.
2. Its responsibility cannot be represented cleanly inside an existing canonical repository.
3. Its ownership and boundaries are unambiguous.
4. Its creation increases clarity rather than fragmentation.

Repositories may be:

- Active
- Experimental
- Archived
- Superseded

Retirement, merger, or division of an institutional repository requires documented review and Founder approval.

Historical records shall be preserved when practical so that future stewards can understand why the architecture changed.

---

# Article XVI — Architectural Constraints

The following rules are binding for Foundation Canon v1.0:

1. Mission precedes architecture.
2. Architecture exists to accelerate the Mission.
3. One institutional responsibility has one canonical home.
4. Projects inherit constitutional governance; they do not rewrite it locally.
5. Cross-references are preferred to uncontrolled duplication.
6. AI capability never creates AI authority.
7. Repository convenience does not override canonical ownership.
8. Architecture shall not be redesigned merely because improvement is possible.
9. Architectural change requires a demonstrated implementation need.
10. The institution must remain understandable to future stewards without requiring the Founder to explain undocumented intent.

---

# Article XVII — Constitutional Test

Every material new initiative shall answer:

1. **Does this advance Helping People Feel Better?**
2. **Which Mission Expression owns it?**
3. **Does a canonical home already exist?**
4. **Is a new repository genuinely necessary?**
5. **Does this require constitutional change, or only implementation?**

If constitutional change is not required, the Constitution remains unchanged.

---

# Article XVIII — Evolution Policy

The Mission is intended to endure.

The architecture is intended to remain stable.

Projects evolve.

Products evolve.

Technology evolves.

AI systems evolve.

Repository implementations evolve.

The constitutional architecture changes only when real institutional experience demonstrates that the current architecture can no longer preserve clarity or effectively support the Mission.

Future improvements that are merely desirable are deferred until there is a demonstrated need.

---

# Article XIX — Success Criteria

FC-001 succeeds when:

- every durable initiative has an obvious institutional home,
- every repository has a distinct purpose,
- every steward understands their authority,
- every canonical artifact has one authoritative location,
- Mission Expressions can work independently without losing institutional coherence,
- new projects can be added without redesigning the institution,
- AI collaborators can orient themselves from repository documentation rather than relying on cached memory,
- the Operating System increasingly disappears into the background while the Mission Expressions produce meaningful work.

---

# Founder Resolution

Finding My Wei exists to preserve and strengthen the work that fulfills the Mission.

The institution shall never become more important than the Mission it serves.

If the institution becomes an obstacle to helping people feel better, it shall be simplified.

The Operating System succeeds when it provides enough clarity, continuity, and stewardship for the Mission Expressions to flourish without repeated architectural redesign.

> **Architecture exists to accelerate the Mission, not delay it.**
