# Purpose

The visual identity of *The Tao of Clinical Touch* should reinforce the work's central qualities: restraint, presence, permission, clinical credibility, spaciousness, and quiet authority.

The brand should not feel loud, over-designed, mystical for its own sake, or conventionally promotional.

---

# Core visual principles

## Restraint
Use visual elements deliberately. Empty space is part of the language.

## Quiet authority
The work should feel thoughtful and established rather than sales-driven.

## Human + clinical
Avoid sterile medical aesthetics and avoid vague wellness clichés. The visual system should sit comfortably between clinical intelligence and human connection.

## Continuity
Publication graphics, email, social assets, event materials, and educational adaptations should visibly belong to the same body of work.

---

# Chapter symbols

The book's chapter symbols are **path signs**, not decorative icons.

They are part of the intellectual and visual language of the Tao and should be preserved in their approved forms.

Do not redraw, embellish, reinterpret, or casually substitute them.

Canonical symbol image assets remain in the repository's legacy brand-asset tree until binary asset migration can be performed without altering or degrading the originals. Git history and the preservation branch protect the source set.

---

# Image handling

When using photography or generated supporting imagery:

- preserve calm composition;
- favor human presence and clinical authenticity;
- avoid exaggerated healing imagery;
- avoid visual claims that exceed the text;
- do not alter documentary photographs in ways that misrepresent people or events;
- allow the words and concepts to remain primary.

---

# Typography and layout

Typography should prioritize readability, editorial sophistication, and restraint.

Layouts should use hierarchy and space rather than excessive ornamentation.

Where an established production template exists, preserve its recognizable system unless a deliberate brand revision is approved.

---

# Relationship to the estate

This is a Tao-specific brand standard and therefore belongs with the Tao domain rather than in the global Operating System.

Shared production mechanics belong in `01_OPERATING_SYSTEM/`.

Tao-specific expression belongs here.

---

# Change control

Material changes to the Tao's visual language require Founder approval.

Routine production may adapt dimensions and composition to a channel while preserving the identity principles above.
