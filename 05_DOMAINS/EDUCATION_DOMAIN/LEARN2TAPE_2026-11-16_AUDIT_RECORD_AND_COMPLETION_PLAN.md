## Lessons to retain
1. Find the actual rendering source. A WordPress post-body save may not alter Elementor-rendered text. Verify the published page.
2. “Saved,” “visible live,” and “works for a student” are separate acceptance levels.
3. Quiz confirmation does not prove a file was uploaded. Instructions must describe the distinction.
4. Passing the written exam is not completion of instructor-reviewed certification.
5. Map real bundle components and historical entitlements before changing downloads.
6. Tool retrieval failure is not evidence of a broken customer link.
7. Reconcile test transactions and refunds before treating analytics as business results.
8. Preserve newest verified outcomes when consolidating an append-only history; do not reopen superseded failures.
These are project lessons, not amendments to institution-wide doctrine.
