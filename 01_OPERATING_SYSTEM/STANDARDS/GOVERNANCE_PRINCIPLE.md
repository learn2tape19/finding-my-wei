# Governance Principle — Human Judgment Over Automation

## Status
Canonical operating standard

## Promoted
September 29, 2026 from the legacy `03_OPERATING_SYSTEM` generation.

---

# Principle

**Automation may accelerate execution, but it must not silently replace human judgment where meaning, permission, identity, safety, intellectual integrity, or consequential commitments are at stake.**

Finding My Wei uses AI and automation as staff functions. Drew Freedman remains the Founder and final authority.

---

# Human approval is required when work materially changes

- intellectual intent or meaning;
- Founder-authored voice or philosophy;
- public claims that have not already been approved;
- audience identity or permission boundaries;
- legal, financial, IP, contractual, or irreversible commitments;
- material deviations from an approved production package;
- strategic priorities or project lifecycle state.

---

# Automation is appropriate for

- mechanical verification;
- reconciliation and completeness checks;
- formatting and bounded transformation;
- scheduling already-approved material;
- retrieval, indexing, organization, and evidence preservation;
- repeatable production steps governed by an approved playbook.

---

# Bounded manual bridge

When automation reaches a technical or permission boundary:

1. Stop at the boundary.
2. Define the smallest human action required.
3. Give the Founder exact instructions.
4. Resume verification afterward when possible.

Do not invent access, bypass controls, or weaken provenance merely to preserve automation.

---

# Evidence standard

The system must distinguish machine verification, Founder confirmation, inference, and unknown state.

Human confirmation is valid evidence when system access prevents independent retrieval, but it must not be mislabeled as machine verification.

---

# Relationship to production

This principle governs `01_OPERATING_SYSTEM/PRODUCTION_PLAYBOOK.md` and all subordinate workflows.

The goal is not maximum automation.

The goal is **reliable execution with preserved human authority**.
