# Purpose

This pipeline governs how raw intellectual material becomes approved knowledge suitable for production.

It is intentionally distinct from `PRODUCTION_PLAYBOOK.md`:

- **Knowledge Production Pipeline:** idea → developed intellectual asset.
- **Production Playbook:** approved asset → deployed and reconciled publication.

This distinction preserves the valuable thinking from the earlier operating system without maintaining a competing second operating system.

---

# Pipeline

## 1. CAPTURE
Record the source idea, observation, question, experience, research input, or Founder insight with provenance.

Do not force premature polish.

## 2. CLARIFY
Identify what the material actually says, why it matters, and what uncertainty remains.

Separate source fact, Founder interpretation, hypothesis, and editorial possibility.

## 3. CONNECT
Relate the material to existing estate knowledge, current projects, research, teaching, products, or publication arcs where the relationship is real.

Do not manufacture connections merely to fill a content calendar.

## 4. DEVELOP
Research, test, outline, draft, synthesize, or otherwise turn the material into a coherent intellectual asset.

## 5. FOUNDER REVIEW
Drew approves the intellectual direction when the material will represent his philosophy, voice, strategic position, or consequential public claim.

## 6. PACKAGE
Prepare the approved intellectual asset for the relevant downstream capability: publication, curriculum, product, decision record, research brief, presentation, or preservation.

## 7. HANDOFF
Once intellectually approved and complete, hand the asset to the appropriate operating playbook or domain workflow.

For publication deployment, use `01_OPERATING_SYSTEM/PRODUCTION_PLAYBOOK.md`.

---

# Quality rules

- Preserve provenance.
- Do not confuse research with Founder belief.
- Do not present AI-generated interpretation as Founder-authored philosophy without approval.
- Prefer meaningful output to content volume.
- Reuse validated knowledge rather than repeatedly rediscovering it.
- Allow incomplete ideas to remain incomplete when further development is not currently valuable.

---

# Stop rule

Not every captured idea must advance.

A knowledge object may remain captured, become dormant, be preserved for later, or be retired if it does not justify additional Founder attention.
