# Purpose

These are durable heuristics for stewarding Finding My Wei. They guide judgment; they are not mandatory maintenance rituals and do not override more specific evidence, Founder direction, or approved playbooks.

---

# Knowledge

- Context travels with conclusions.
- Preserve provenance so future stewards can distinguish source, interpretation, hypothesis, and decision.
- Teaching can reveal whether an idea is actually understood.
- Repeated patterns are worth capturing when they improve future judgment.

# Decisions

- Record consequential reasoning, not merely the final choice.
- State important assumptions and tradeoffs explicitly.
- Preserve meaningful dissent or uncertainty rather than rewriting history as inevitable.
- Revisit decisions when context materially changes; do not create review work merely because a calendar interval elapsed.

# Communication

- Clear writing usually exposes clear thinking.
- Name things carefully because names become navigation.
- Prefer useful connections and canonical references to duplicated explanation.
- Attribution matters.

# Collaboration

- Complementary strengths are more valuable than duplicated roles.
- Clarity is more useful than artificial agreement.
- AI roles are functional and may change as tools change; Founder authority does not.
- Process should reduce coordination friction rather than create administrative theater.

# Creation

- Constraints can focus creative work.
- Iteration generally improves ideas when feedback is meaningful.
- Rest and dormancy are legitimate parts of stewardship.
- Not every captured idea must become an output.

# Sustainability

- Prefer systems that can be maintained for years over systems optimized for short bursts of activity.
- Small improvements in clarity, quality, and efficiency compound.
- Meaning and mission should constrain commercial optimization rather than be subordinated to it.

# Technology

- Tools serve purpose; tools are not doctrine.
- Preserve important institutional assets in formats and locations the estate can control and recover.
- Automate stable repetition when automation actually reduces friction.
- Do not automate judgment merely because a task can be automated.

# Growth

- Deep mastery matters more than indiscriminate expansion.
- Setbacks are evidence when examined without distortion.
- Relationships compound and deserve stewardship.
- Evaluate long-horizon work over an appropriately long horizon.

---

# Cadence rule

The June source prescribed frequent recurring review in several places. This canonical version replaces calendar compulsion with a consequence-based rule:

> **Review when stakes, change, uncertainty, or learning justify attention. Otherwise permit dormancy.**

Quarterly or annual stewardship reviews may be useful, but they are not proof of institutional health by themselves.
