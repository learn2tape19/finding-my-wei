# Purpose

Finding My Wei manages knowledge as a living estate. Not every idea deserves publication, not every project deserves continuous activity, and not every historical artifact should remain operational.

This standard defines how knowledge moves without confusing preservation with perpetual work.

---

# Knowledge lifecycle

## CAPTURE
A thought, source, observation, artifact, conversation, or decision enters the estate.

Preserve provenance. Do not over-organize prematurely.

## DEVELOP
Material is clarified, researched, connected, tested, or shaped into useful knowledge.

## VALIDATE
Important claims, decisions, or production packages receive the level of review appropriate to their consequence.

## DEPLOY
Approved knowledge becomes a publication, product, curriculum, decision, operating standard, campaign, or other usable output.

## PRESERVE
Enduring knowledge is stored with enough context and provenance to remain intelligible later.

## DORMANT
A valid asset or project may intentionally require no current activity. Dormancy does not diminish its intellectual value.

## RETIRE / ARCHIVE
Obsolete operating machinery is removed from current authority while historically meaningful material remains recoverable.

---

# Core distinctions

**Preserved does not mean active.**

**Active does not mean more important.**

**Archived does not mean discarded.**

**Dormant does not mean failed.**

---

# Promotion rule

Material should become canonical only when real use, Founder approval, legal/contractual necessity, or enduring intellectual value justifies the additional authority.

Do not promote drafts merely because they are well formatted.

---

# Retirement rule

A workflow, document, or system may be retired when:

- its function has a verified canonical successor;
- its unique knowledge has been preserved;
- active dependencies have been checked;
- retirement will not erase contractual, financial, IP, historical, or evidentiary value.

Git history may serve as preservation when duplicating the full file tree would create unnecessary clutter.

---

# Stewardship cadence

Current operating state should be reviewed when reality materially changes and, for intentionally quiet work, during periodic stewardship review.

The system does not require cosmetic updates simply to appear active.
