# Finding My Wei — Operating State

## Date
September 29, 2026

## Purpose

This document answers one question:

**Where should Founder attention go now?**

It does not rank the enduring value of the Intellectual Estate. It describes the present operating state of its major domains and projects.

---

# Operating States

## ACTIVE
Requires current Founder decisions, execution, or sustained forward motion.

## MAINTAIN
Has ongoing value and should remain healthy, but should consume bounded Founder attention.

## DORMANT
Preserved and intentionally inactive. No recurring work is required until a defined trigger reactivates it.

## SUNSET / PRESERVE
Active operations have ended. Preserve the intellectual, historical, contractual, or legacy value without maintaining an unnecessary operating machine.

# Attention Principle

**Asset importance and current Founder attention are different things.**

Finding My Wei may preserve every major domain with equal seriousness while allocating Founder time unequally.

The estate protects what matters across decades.

The operating state protects what matters this week.

---

# Dormancy Rule

DORMANT is a healthy state, not a failure state.

When a project becomes dormant:

- No recurring maintenance is presumed.
- No status document must be updated merely to prove activity.
- No AI collaborator should generate work simply because the project exists.
- Existing knowledge remains discoverable and preserved.
- Reactivation requires a meaningful trigger: new evidence, opportunity, obligation, risk, customer need, partner action, or Founder decision.
