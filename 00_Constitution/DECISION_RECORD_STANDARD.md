# Institutional Decision Record Standard

## Status
Founder-approved constitutional governance standard

## Purpose
Finding My Wei preserves not only what was decided, but why, under what authority, and what the decision superseded.

A permanent decision record is required when a choice materially changes governance, an authoritative standard, project or publication direction, system architecture, significant spending, customer or audience experience, public promises or policies, or a previously approved institutional precedent.

## Required fields

- **ID** — unique durable identifier
- **Date** — date of decision
- **Decision owner** — person holding approval authority
- **Context** — problem or condition requiring decision
- **Authority consulted** — governing documents, approved precedents, or evidence
- **Options considered** — meaningful alternatives
- **Decision** — approved choice
- **Reasoning** — why this option was selected
- **Consequences** — expected effects, constraints, or obligations
- **Review trigger** — condition that should cause reconsideration
- **Supersedes / superseded by** — explicit lineage when prior authority changes

## Authority rule
Conversation, AI memory, draft language, and project-local interpretation may inform a decision but do not become authority merely through repetition.

Once approved and recorded, the decision record becomes part of the institutional authority chain at the scope granted by the Founder.

## Supersession rule
New decisions do not silently erase old decisions. When a decision changes prior authority, the relationship must be recorded explicitly.

## Promotion rule
A project-level decision becomes institution-wide doctrine only when it satisfies the Institutional Promotion Rule and receives Founder approval.

## Preservation principle
Future collaborators should be able to determine:

1. what was decided;
2. who had authority to decide it;
3. why it was decided;
4. what evidence or precedent informed it;
5. whether it remains active;
6. what it replaced, if anything.
