# DR-2026-09-29 — Repository Reconciliation and Simplification

## Date
September 29, 2026

## Decision owner
Drew Freedman, Founder

## Status
Active

---

## Context

Finding My Wei accumulated multiple architectural generations: case-variant constitutional roots, competing operating-system locations, March AI/status layers, June cognitive engines, and a later production system that proved itself through real publication work.

The repository had documented its own architectural debt but had not completed the transition. The result was multiple files and directories capable of appearing authoritative at the same time.

A September review identified the need to bless the working production system, collapse duplicate architecture, add healthy dormancy/rest, and reduce succession complexity.

---

## Authority consulted

- Repository Constitution v2.0
- Constitution Layer Charter
- Founder-approved Integration over Reorganization principle
- Institutional Decision Record Standard
- September 2026 operating evidence, including the proven Issue production workflow
- Founder approvals in the September 29 reconciliation session

---

## Options considered

### Preserve all generations in place
Rejected because historical preservation was being confused with current authority and continued to impose navigation and maintenance cost.

### Replace everything with a new architecture
Rejected because the repository already contains working systems and valuable doctrine. Another redesign would repeat the problem.

### Reconcile selectively, promote durable doctrine, preserve history in Git, and physically retire superseded live surfaces
Chosen.

---

## Decision

Finding My Wei will maintain one canonical constitutional layer, one canonical reusable Operating System, one canonical current-state surface, and bounded domain/project/campaign homes.

Historical architectural generations may remain recoverable through Git without remaining visible as live operating systems.

The reconciliation is authorized to remove superseded files on the isolated reconciliation branch after their unique knowledge, authority, and canonical successor have been accounted for.

Active proven production is protected from cosmetic reorganization.

Dormancy is recognized as a legitimate operating state; recurring maintenance is not required merely because an asset exists.

---

## Consequences

- `00_Constitution/` is the canonical constitutional layer.
- `01_OPERATING_SYSTEM/` is the canonical reusable operating layer.
- `OPERATING_STATE.md` governs present attention/state.
- The proven publication Production Playbook is explicitly canonical.
- Parallel and obsolete top-level engines may be retired after reconciliation.
- Git history and the preservation branch carry exact historical recovery.
- Review cadence becomes consequence-based rather than compulsory.
- Tao-specific brand doctrine/assets belong with Tao rather than a global operating-system generation.

---

## Review trigger

Revisit this decision if:

- the Founder formally amends the constitutional architecture;
- a retired system is shown to contain unique unreconciled institutional knowledge;
- a canonical successor proves insufficient in real operation;
- the repository again develops multiple competing truth surfaces.

Routine calendar passage alone is not a review trigger.

---

## Supersedes / superseded by

This decision supersedes earlier architectural assumptions that required preservation of every historical operating surface as a live top-level layer, including compulsory daily/weekly/monthly/quarterly cognitive-engine maintenance.

It does not erase the historical source documents; they remain recoverable through Git.

No superseding decision at time of record.
