# DR-2026-10-12: Work production and institutional preservation

**ID:** DR-2026-10-12-WORK-PRODUCTION-AND-PRESERVATION  
**Date:** October 12, 2026  
**Decision owner:** Drew Freedman, Founder  
**Status:** Active; Founder-directed operating preference  
**Scope:** Tao and other Finding My Wei projects where the tools, access and required review roles support it.

## Context

Drew identified Issue 016 as the first Tao issue built primarily in Work. Earlier issues were developed in Chat and handed to Claude for proofing and execution. Drew found the Work process much simpler and explicitly requested retention for future Tao tasks and other projects.

A subsequent review found that the closeout summary had been committed while the reusable lesson, detailed receipts and approved image originals remained local. Production closure had also been expressed without an explicit standardized publication status. This is an institutional preservation failure, not a reason to recreate approved work.

## Authority consulted

- Institutional Learning Principle: institutional memory precedes individual memory.
- Institutional Execution Standard: one source, smallest complete correction, evidence-backed QA and reproducibility.
- Institutional Decision Record Standard.
- September 29 Repository Reconciliation decision: protect proven production and integrate rather than create competing architecture.
- Production Playbook and publication/campaign authority registers.
- Drew's October 12 instructions to retain this lesson across projects, close and commit Issue 016, and make the accommodations required to stay aligned.

## Options considered

1. Keep using the Chat-to-Claude handoff by default. Rejected as an unnecessary default when Work can carry the approved flow.
2. Preserve learning only in AI memory and record only a completion summary. Rejected because future collaborators cannot inherit authoritative evidence.
3. Use Work for the complete flow when appropriate, preserve meaningful review roles and Founder gates, and integrate lessons and evidence into existing repository records. Chosen.

## Decision

Default to keeping production, proofing, review, Founder approval, execution, verification and closeout within Work when practical. Tool consolidation does not collapse required scientific/editorial review roles, remove approval gates or authorize future publishing without task-specific approval. Use another tool or a bounded Founder manual bridge when it materially improves the outcome or resolves an access boundary.

No governed task is institutionally complete from local memory or a closeout claim alone. Before saying the repository is complete, preserve approved artifacts and reusable decisions, update indexes and current authority, commit and push, read back the remote state, and certify the isolated working tree. Report unresolved criteria as NOT VERIFIABLE or HOLD, not PASS.

## Reasoning

Issue 016 demonstrated a simpler flow by Founder observation. This is a qualitative workflow result; no measured time or cost savings are asserted. The preservation gap demonstrates why execution and institutional completion must be checked separately.

The goal is not to avoid all mistakes. It is to recognize a mistake, retain what it teaches, improve the method, and avoid repeating the same failure. Learn, retain, evolve and continue to aspire.

## Consequences

- Existing Production Playbook gains the reusable Work and preservation rules.
- Editorial decision log and campaign ledger reference this decision.
- Approved Issue 016 originals and detailed receipts are retained in its repository evidence package.
- Issue 016 article has one canonical BLOG home; the old issue path is a pointer.
- Issue 016 production/scheduling remains closed; publication is READY TO PUBLISH until verified live, then PUBLISHED; ARCHIVED requires later reflection.
- Existing approval and quality gates continue. Platform schedules and approved article text are unchanged.

## Review trigger

Reconsider the tool preference if access, scientific/editorial independence, capability, quality or reproducibility makes the consolidated flow unsuitable. Reopen an affected acceptance criterion when new evidence contradicts it. Calendar passage alone is not a trigger to redesign the workflow.

## Supersedes / superseded by

Supersedes any assumption that a Chat-to-Claude handoff is required by default or that local AI memory constitutes institutional preservation. It does not supersede constitutional authority, review independence, asset integrity, public publishing approval or brand/audience boundaries. No superseding record at time of decision.
