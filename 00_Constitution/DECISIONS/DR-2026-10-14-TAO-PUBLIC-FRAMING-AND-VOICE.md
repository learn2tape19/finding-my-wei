# Tao public framing and voice continuity

**ID:** DR-2026-10-14-TAO-PUBLIC-FRAMING-AND-VOICE

**Date:** October 14, 2026

**Decision owner:** Drew Freedman, Founder

**Status:** Active Founder direction; revised wording remains unapproved

## Context

After approving SR01 direction, Drew stated that Taylor understands his voice best and that the material should not be acknowledged as recycled: few had read it and it will be new to most readers. When asked where Taylor's guidance is held, he answered “in /findingmywei repo”.

## Authority consulted

Founder’s direct correction; `00_EXECUTIVE/DAILY_FOUNDER_BRIEF.md` operating-model section (Taylor: Architect / Executive Chief of Staff / Knowledge Synthesizer); `01_OPERATING_SYSTEM/PUBLISHING/VOICE_GUIDE.md`; `EDITORIAL_STANDARDS.md`; existing editorial review and Founder gates; prior SR01 direction and GitHub continuity decisions.

## Options considered

Public Second Reading/revisit framing, or standalone clinical-topic presentation with historical lineage retained internally. Founder directed the latter.

## Decision

- Keep Second Reading and SR/original-issue mappings as internal campaign lineage.
- Present new public copy and artwork through the clinical topic. Remove rereading, returning, recycled-material and original-versus-new explanations from the new treatments.
- Do not imply that original articles were newly published or alter their dates, bodies, titles, URLs or approved assets.
- Ground voice work in Taylor's institutional context and the existing repo voice/editorial standards. Taylor is the Founder-preferred voice authority; do not substitute an operator self-check for an independent review or invent Taylor approval.

## Reasoning

Founder reports the original readership was small and expects the material to be new to the majority. Topic-led presentation serves that audience without explaining internal campaign reuse.

## Consequences

SR01 reflection, email, captions, Story sequence and artwork brief revised to v02 for review. SR02–SR04 treatments with old framing are held until revised. Dates, destinations, internal tracking and original publication sources remain intact. No messaging to Taylor, separate-agent review, scientific clearance, final copy/visual approval or release is claimed.

## Review trigger

Taylor/Founder voice feedback or a change to public framing. Subsequent revisions require exact-version approval under the existing workflow.

## Supersedes / superseded by

Supersedes public Second Reading branding and read-again language in the initial pilot proposal and the presented SR01 framing. Retains the approved SR01 permission angle and cadence, original-source preservation and all review/release gates. No superseding decision at creation.
