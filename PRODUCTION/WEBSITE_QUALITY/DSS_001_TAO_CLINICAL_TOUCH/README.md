## Overview

This directory contains the complete governance record for Digital Stewardship Sprint 001, following the five-gate model established in the Digital Stewardship Standard:

1. **Gate 0 — Observation** ✓
   - Public crawl and baseline establishment
   - Factual inventory of all public content
   - Zero modifications
   - **Status:** COMPLETE

2. **Gate 1 — Understanding** ⏳
   - Editorial alignment audit
   - Technical and accessibility assessment
   - Treatment plan development
   - Complete audit documentation
   - **Status:** AUTHORIZED (proceed immediately)

3. **Gate 2 — Consensus** ⏳
   - Founder reviews and approves Treatment Plan
   - Architect validates governance alignment
   - Steward confirms technical feasibility
   - **Status:** PENDING Gate 1 completion

4. **Gate 3 — Intervention** ⏳
   - Execute approved changes in small, verified phases
   - Document every modification
   - Verify after each phase
   - **Status:** NOT AUTHORIZED until Consensus is reached

5. **Gate 4 — Reflection** ⏳
   - Compare against baseline
   - Measure improvement
   - Document institutional knowledge
   - Inform future stewardship
   - **Status:** PENDING completion of Intervention

## Governance Principles

This engagement is governed by:

- **Constitutional Principle** — Digital properties are public expressions of the institution
- **North Star** — A first-time visitor immediately understands what the Tao is, who Drew is, why the work is different, how ideas connect, where to go next
- **Principle of Invitation** — Reduce friction between curiosity and understanding; invite through clarity
- **Principle of Minimal Necessary Change** — Only change what needs to change to strengthen clarity, credibility, accessibility, performance, discoverability, or institutional coherence
- **Principle of Successive Stewardship** — Every change is reversible, documented, justified, and verified
- **Principle of Understanding Before Intervention** — Complete understanding precedes all changes
- **Principle of Consensus Before Action** — Founder, Architect, and Steward must align before modifications occur
