## Wednesday — ARRIVAL IS ASSESSMENT
**Canonical line:** Observe first. Interpret later.

**Facebook**  
Assessment begins before the first question. Before range of motion. Before palpation. Before orthopedic testing. It begins with another human being arriving. Observation is not about reaching faster conclusions. It's about giving ourselves permission to reach better ones. **Issue No. 006 — Before Touch**
