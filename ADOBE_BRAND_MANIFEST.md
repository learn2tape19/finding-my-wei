## 4. Brand Marks & Graphics Registry

### Primary Mark: IA-001 Canonical Ripple
**Source File:** tao_ripple.png  
**Format:** PNG (transparent background)  
**Composition:** Natural water ripple with concentric circles, asymmetric, minimal  
**Meaning:** One therapeutic contact; one clinical moment; one point of influence changing an entire system  
**Scale Behavior:**
- **Masthead:** 60×60px, quiet and immediately recognizable
- **Ivory layouts:** Faint, embossed or nearly submerged ripple field
- **Navy layouts:** Fine concentric ripple field with restrained contrast
- **Testimonial layouts:** Radiates behind or outward from quotation
- **Covers & features:** Larger structural field, never visual noise

### Ripple Variants (To be generated)
- **Masthead-scale variant:** 60×60px minimum, tight bounds
- **Ivory-background variant:** Faint, embossed treatment
- **Navy-background variant:** Fine concentric field with restrained contrast

**Prohibited:** No Enso, Tao brush circle, painted ring, calligraphy, yin-yang, bamboo, spa clichés, loud photographic splash, or glowing orb.
