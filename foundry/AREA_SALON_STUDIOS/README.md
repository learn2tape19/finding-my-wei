## Direction

AREA work should improve qualified demand, tours, sustainable occupancy, the owner/community experience, or operating reliability. Current marketing direction is captured in the [November 12 brief](https://chatgpt.com/library?file_id=libfile_e9216ffc412c8191be57b9a1f66742af&account_id=personal); it supersedes older inventory assumptions. AREA HQ retains the prospect and licensee record.

The system should make it possible to find a piece, identify its approved version, recover its source/export and exact copy, understand its actual distribution history, and reuse it deliberately. A filename containing `FINAL`, a stored export or a schedule alone is not proof of approval or publication.
