# Why this generation was retired

The `_system/` directory was an early AI-centered execution environment built around OpenClaw/Taylor Swain, daily TODAY files, three-project balancing, and Notion mirroring.

It served a real purpose in March 2026, but by September the estate had materially evolved:

- Tao had been published and had an active issue-production system.
- Sidekick Air had advanced into production-reference development and commercialization work.
- Learn2Tape remained an operating commercial asset.
- Boston Bodyworker had become a legacy/preservation asset.
- Finding My Wei had acquired a ratified Constitution, a formal IEMS architecture, current operating-state doctrine, and a proven production playbook.

Keeping `_system/` visible as a current system therefore created false authority and stale instructions.

# Principles preserved from this generation

The reconciliation retains several durable ideas without retaining stale machinery:

- Prefer execution to organizational theater.
- Keep priorities bounded.
- Tie work to outcomes.
- Surface Founder decisions clearly.
- Avoid unnecessary parallel documents.
- Preserve explicit human approval for consequential actions.
- Use AI to reduce friction rather than create administrative work.

These principles now operate under the current Constitution, `OPERATING_STATE.md`, `START_HERE.md`, and `01_OPERATING_SYSTEM/`.

---

# Important supersessions

The following legacy rules are explicitly no longer authoritative:

- Every project must receive a meaningful touch every 48 hours.
- The estate must always focus on exactly three projects.
- Tao may receive no new content.
- Work must originate from `_system/TODAY.md`.
- Notion is a required daily mirror.
- OpenClaw/Taylor is the master execution engine.

Current doctrine permits unequal attention and healthy dormancy.
