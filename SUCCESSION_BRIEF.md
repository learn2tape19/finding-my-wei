# Finding My Wei — Succession Brief

## Status
Founder-level continuity document

## Purpose

This is the practical entry point for a future steward if Drew Freedman is unavailable, chooses to step back, or transfers responsibility for part of the estate.

It is not a substitute for the underlying repository. It tells a successor what matters enough to investigate first.

---

# First Principle

**Preserve meaning before preserving machinery.**

Do not keep a process alive merely because documentation exists for it.

Protect intellectual property, contractual obligations, revenue-producing assets, published work, customer/student obligations, relationships, provenance, and the Founder’s documented intent.

---

# What Matters

## 1. StitchCore / Sidekick Air

Potentially significant product and intellectual-property value.

A successor should immediately establish:

- Current IP and trademark status
- Engineering and prototype state
- Manufacturing relationships and obligations
- Ownership of CAD, specifications, tooling, prototypes, and production-reference work
- Outstanding payments or contractual commitments
- Commercial relationships and serious market opportunities

Do not make irreversible product or IP decisions without understanding the complete record.

## 2. Learn2Tape

Existing education and commercial infrastructure.

Establish:

- Current products and pricing
- LMS/course access
- Student and CE obligations
- Payment and ecommerce infrastructure
- Email/audience systems
- Material partnerships
- Revenue and recurring operating costs

Maintain customer and learner obligations before pursuing growth.

## 3. The Tao of Clinical Touch

Published intellectual property and active authority platform.

Establish:

- Publication and distribution status
- Website and mailing infrastructure
- Copyright and source files
- Current publication pipeline
- Educational adoption and institutional relationships
- Pending commitments to readers, educators, reviewers, or partners

Preserve the Founder’s voice. Do not use AI to fabricate new Drew-authored philosophy after Drew is no longer directing it.

## 4. The Boston Bodyworker

Professional legacy and intellectual asset.

Preserve the clinical writing, professional history, important relationships, website/SEO value where appropriate, and historical record.

Do not recreate the former clinical business merely because the archive exists.

## 5. Finding My Wei / Intellectual Estate

Preserve Git history and the repository itself.

The estate contains provenance that may be more valuable than any individual folder: why decisions were made, how ideas evolved, and which assets are canonical.

---

# What Can Be Stopped

Unless a contractual, financial, customer, legal, safety, or preservation obligation requires otherwise, a successor may stop:

- Routine status production
- Cosmetic documentation maintenance
- Dormant automations
- Experimental workflows with no continuing value
- Duplicate systems
- Publishing cadences that no longer serve a real audience
- AI activity performed only to demonstrate activity

Stopping a process does not require destroying its history.

---

# Money and Obligations

The repository is not guaranteed to contain a complete financial ledger.

A successor must independently identify current bank, payment processor, ecommerce, tax, insurance, vendor, subscription, contract, royalty, receivable, and payable obligations through the appropriate secure systems.

Credentials, secrets, API keys, and private financial information should not be placed in the public repository.

---

# Decision Order for a Successor

When uncertain:

1. Protect people and outstanding obligations.
2. Protect legal rights and intellectual property.
3. Protect access to revenue-producing systems and records.
4. Preserve canonical source material and provenance.
5. Stabilize active commitments.
6. Stop unnecessary recurring machinery.
7. Only then consider new growth.

---

# Where to Start

Read, in order:

1. `START_HERE.md`
2. `OPERATING_STATE.md`
3. `00_Constitution/CONSTITUTION.md`
4. The current domain/project records relevant to any active obligation
5. Recent Git history and execution/decision receipts

Do not attempt to read the entire estate sequentially.

Use the repository as a reference system, following the active question into the relevant evidence.

During Drew Freedman's active stewardship, this brief does not transfer authority away from him.
