1. Original full statement and exact continuous excerpt with surrounding meaning.
2. Approved attribution and any credentials actually used.
3. Permission covering this paid Meta campaign, organic/website uses as applicable, named attribution, edits/excerpts, duration and territory.
4. Image rights if a photograph is proposed; first versions can be text/cover only.
5. Relevant supplied-copy, friendship or other material-connection context and appropriate disclosure.
6. Founder approval of exact quote and composition.
