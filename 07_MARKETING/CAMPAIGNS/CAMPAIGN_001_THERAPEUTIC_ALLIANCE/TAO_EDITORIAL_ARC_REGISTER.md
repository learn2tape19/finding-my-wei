## 1. Purpose

This register prevents daily and weekly Tao production from starting with amnesia.

Before any new Tao article, social derivative, visual, email, or execution packet is produced, the operator must resolve:

1. Where the publication has already taken the reader.
2. What the current issue is responsible for advancing.
3. What has already been taught and should not be repeated as though it were new.
4. What conceptual step the requested day must add.
5. What the requested day must hand forward to the next day or issue.

The governing production hierarchy is:

> **Publication Arc → Issue Arc → Daily Arc → Canonical Source → Derivatives → Visuals → Founder Approval → Execution**

A request such as **“Build Wednesday Issue 007”** does not authorize an isolated Wednesday idea. It first triggers this continuity gate.

---

## 2. Authority Labels

Use these labels when the historical record is uneven:

- **CONFIRMED** — directly established by approved/canonical campaign records or Founder-approved production.
- **HISTORICAL** — accurately records an earlier production state, including incomplete cadence or recovery conditions.
- **PROVISIONAL** — continuity inference supported by the established arc but not yet Founder-approved as canonical public copy.
- **UNRESOLVED** — competing or insufficient evidence exists; do not silently choose a version.

The register must preserve history rather than retroactively making every week look uniform.

# 3. Publication-Level Story Arc

The first seven issues form a cumulative clinical argument.

### Issue 001 — The Invisible Beginning
**Move:** Reframe clinical outcomes away from technique alone and toward the conditions in which technique is received.

### Issue 002 — The Greet Is the First Clinical Intervention
**Move:** Bring the invisible beginning into the first observable moment of the session.

### Issue 003 — The Tao of Assessment: Perception Before Interpretation
**Move:** Show that the conditions established in the greet affect what becomes observable during assessment.

### Issue 004 — The Tao of Listening: Listening Before Explanation
**Move:** Extend assessment into meaning. Information becomes clinically useful when the clinician listens before explaining and allows what is heard to change the plan.

### Issue 005 — Clinical Understanding Changes Clinical Decisions
**Move:** Convert listening and observation into clinical reasoning: question → expectation → pattern → synthesis → decision.

### Issue 006 — Before Touch
**Move:** Widen the clinical field. The room, arrival, pause, and relationship are already communicating before the first hands-on intervention.

### Issue 007 — Permission Over Time
**Move:** Advance permission from an initial yes into an ongoing clinical conversation that can change as the experience changes.

### Cumulative staircase

> **Relationship → Orientation → Perception → Listening → Understanding → Conditions → Ongoing Permission**

Operational shorthand:

> **Notice → Understand → Prepare → Keep Listening → Respond**

The publication must continue climbing this staircase. New content should not descend to an earlier step and present it as a new insight unless the purpose is deliberate integration.

## ISSUE 002 — THE GREET IS THE FIRST CLINICAL INTERVENTION

**Status:** CONFIRMED

**Manuscript anchor:** Chapter 2, *The Tao of the Greet: Regulation Before Inquiry*

### Governing idea
The greet is not administrative time before the clinical work. It is where the clinical work begins.

### Reader transformation
By the end of the issue, the reader should move through:

> recognition → self-awareness → orientation/predictability → clinical consequence → self-reflection

### Daily arc

**Monday — The session begins before touch**  
Core line: **The greet is the first clinical intervention.**  
Role: Establish the reframe. The patient arrives carrying the momentum of their day, history, uncertainty, and pain.

**Tuesday — The practitioner is part of the environment**  
Core line: **Your patient experiences your pace before they experience your technique.**  
Role: Turn attention toward clinician state and nonverbal signals without shaming imperfection.

**Wednesday — Every clinician has a beard**  
Core line: Every clinician has a signal they may not realize the patient is reading.  
Role: Personal self-audit of pace, tone, posture, lighting, silence, room setup, intensity, and unexplained transitions.

**Thursday — Canonical article**  
Title: **The Greet Is the First Clinical Intervention**  
Role: Teach orientation before interrogation, observation without performance, listening without steering, and predictability before assessment/touch.

**Friday — Better data begins with less uncertainty**  
Role: Connect the greet directly to clinical reasoning. A guarded system may show protection; a more settled system may reveal different information about capacity.

### What Issue 002 established
The clinician and environment influence what the patient brings into assessment. The session begins before formal testing and before touch.

### What it handed forward
If the greet changes the conditions of observation, then assessment cannot be treated as context-free.

> **What are you actually assessing?**

That question produces Issue 003.

## ISSUE 003 — THE TAO OF ASSESSMENT: PERCEPTION BEFORE INTERPRETATION

**Status:** CONFIRMED / HISTORICAL RECOVERY WEEK

**Manuscript anchor:** Chapter 3, *The Tao of Assessment: Perception Before Interpretation*

### Governing idea
Assessment is listening before acting. Its meaning depends not only on the test selected, but on the conditions under which the finding appeared.

### Campaign bridge
The greet creates the conditions for assessment. Assessment must preserve those conditions.

### Historical cadence
Week 03 began late. The campaign explicitly prohibited backdating a missed Monday. The issue therefore used a controlled recovery sequence rather than pretending a normal Monday launch occurred.

**Tuesday — Assessment Begins Before the Test**  
Role: Bridge Issue 002 into assessment. Preserve the conditions established during the greet.

**Wednesday — Perception before interpretation / observation**  
Role: Separate what is observed from the meaning assigned to it. History, movement, touch, and context are inputs, not instant conclusions.

**Thursday — Canonical article**  
Title: **What Are You Actually Assessing?**  
Role: Teach the full idea. Formal tests matter, but findings must be understood within context, readiness, pacing, and clinician restraint.

**Friday — The Two-Breath Pause**  
Primary line: **A pause can sometimes clarify what more pressure would only complicate.**  
Role: Give the clinician one practical observation behavior without turning it into a diagnostic test.

### What Issue 003 established
Observation and interpretation are separate clinical acts. The clinician is part of the assessment field, and a finding is meaningful only in relation to the conditions under which it emerged.

### What it handed forward
If better assessment requires better receiving, then the clinician must become more disciplined about listening before explaining.

That produces Issue 004.

## ISSUE 005 — CLINICAL UNDERSTANDING CHANGES CLINICAL DECISIONS

**Status:** CONFIRMED

### Governing idea
Every clinical decision begins before touch. What we notice, assume, ask, expect, and synthesize determines which intervention makes sense and when.

### Daily arc

**Monday — THE QUESTION**  
Feature: **The Decision Before the Decision**  
Anchor: **The right question changes everything.**  
Role: Examine the clinical lens brought into the room before hands make contact.

**Tuesday — THE EXPECTATION**  
Anchor: **Expectation shapes perception.**  
Role: Ask what expectations entered the room before interpretation began.

**Wednesday — THE PATTERN**  
Anchor: **Patterns are observations. Not conclusions.**  
Role: Protect pattern recognition from becoming premature certainty.

**Thursday — THE SYNTHESIS**  
Anchor: **Information becomes understanding only after reflection.**  
Role: Move from collected inputs toward coherent clinical meaning.

**Friday — THE DECISION**  
Anchor: **Understanding changes treatment.**  
Role: Close the reasoning loop by showing that better understanding should alter what the clinician does next.

### What Issue 005 established
Clinical reasoning is not a pile of observations. It is a disciplined progression from information to meaning to choice.

### What it handed forward
If clinical decisions are shaped before touch, then the publication must look more closely at the conditions that exist before the first intervention.

That produces Issue 006.

## ISSUE 006 — BEFORE TOUCH

**Status:** CONFIRMED

### Governing idea
Clinical work begins before contact. The environment, arrival, pace, attention, pause, and existing relationship shape the conditions into which technique enters.

### Daily arc

**Monday — THE WORK BEFORE THE WORK**  
Role: Preparation before contact. Clinical excellence includes what happens before anyone is touched.

**Tuesday — THE ROOM SPEAKS FIRST**  
Role: Every clinical environment communicates before the practitioner does.

**Wednesday — ARRIVAL IS ASSESSMENT**  
Role: Observation begins with another human being arriving. Observe first; interpret later.

**Thursday — THE PAUSE BEFORE TECHNIQUE**  
Role: Stillness is disciplined attention. A pause allows first impressions and assumptions to soften before intervention.

**Friday — THE FIRST TOUCH WAS NEVER THE FIRST INTERVENTION**  
Role: Touch joins a relationship and clinical experience already in progress.

### Week synthesis

> **Prepare → environment communicates → observe arrival → pause → touch joins what already began**

### What Issue 006 established
The clinical field exists before technique. Touch does not create the therapeutic relationship from nothing; it enters conditions already being shaped.

### What it handed forward
Once touch begins, the clinician cannot stop listening. The person’s experience can continue to change.

That produces Issue 007.

## ISSUE 007 — PERMISSION OVER TIME

**Status:** ACTIVE / PARTIALLY CONFIRMED

### Governing idea
Permission is not captured once at the beginning of an encounter. A yes can begin the experience, but the person remains free to communicate something different as pressure, position, sensation, expectation, and meaning change.

### Confirmed Monday

**Monday — PERMISSION IS NOT A QUESTION**  
Confirmed language:

> **A yes can begin an encounter. It cannot speak for everything that follows.**

Monday’s three-frame Story structure further establishes:

1. Permission can begin with yes.
2. The experience can change: pressure, position, sensation, expectation.
3. Permission must be able to change with the experience.

Recovered supporting concepts:

- guarding can appear;
- movement can shift;
- conversation can quiet;
- not every change means stop;

### Wednesday — continuity assignment

**Status:** PROVISIONAL — headline/article must pass Founder approval before production.

Wednesday’s job is now constrained by Issues 001–006 and Monday–Tuesday of Issue 007:

- It must **not** re-teach that permission matters.
- It must **not** reduce permission to repeated verbal consent questions.
- It must advance from **“the experience can change”** to **“change is information the clinician must remain available to notice.”**
- It should locate permission in the evolving clinical encounter: breath, guarding, movement, tone, silence, participation, language, and other context-dependent signals.
- It must preserve the distinction that a change is information, not automatically a command to stop and not a diagnostic conclusion.

**Wednesday conceptual step:**

> **A different answer may appear before it is spoken.**

This is a continuity assignment, not yet approved public headline copy.

### Thursday — provisional role
Move from noticing change to responding without defensiveness, urgency, or therapist ego. The practitioner’s task is to make room for the changed answer to alter what happens next.

### Friday — provisional role
Integrate the week: ongoing permission is relational and clinical. It is not an administrative checkbox, and it is not constant interrogation. It is the continuing ability of the person’s experience to influence the encounter.

### What Issue 007 should establish
Clinical permission is dynamic. The clinician’s responsibility does not end when initial consent is obtained; attention must remain responsive as the experience unfolds.

### What Issue 007 should hand forward
