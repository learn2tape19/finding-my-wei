The question is not whether we have a clinical lens. We do.

The question is whether we can recognize when that lens has become the decision.

## Wednesday — From Information to Understanding

### Instagram

Information is not yet understanding.

A client can give us accurate details, and we can collect useful findings, but meaning emerges only when those details are understood in context.

The nervous system does not process data in isolation. It organizes experience through safety, expectation, memory, and relationship.

Better assessment is not simply gathering more. It is learning to recognize what the information means for this person, in this moment.

## Thursday — Decision Quality Is Patient Safety

### Instagram

Patient safety is not limited to avoiding injury.

It includes the quality of the decisions that shape care.

When interpretation outruns understanding, even technically competent work can become poorly matched to the person receiving it.

A better decision begins with a better understanding—of the client's experience, goals, readiness, and response.

Precision is not only where we place our hands. It is how carefully we decide what should happen next.
