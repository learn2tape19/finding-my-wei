## Core Message Architecture (Approved)

### Primary Anchor
**LISTENING IS A CLINICAL INTERVENTION.**

### Supporting Line
**Before you explain, make room for what matters.**

### Governing Idea
Listening is not the pause before the clinician speaks. It is part of the clinical process.

Before a clinician explains, corrects, tests, or touches, the patient is already communicating through words, pace, hesitation, posture, breath, and what they choose not to say. The clinician's task is not to extract a better story. It is to create enough room for the useful story to emerge.

### Desired Reader Response
"I do this—but I may move to interpretation or explanation sooner than I realize."

## Visual System (Social Visual Doctrine v1.0)

### Canonical Approach

All Week 4 Monday social visuals use the Founder-approved Canonical Social Visual Doctrine v1.0 (July 27, 2026). This doctrine governs charcoal/navy tonal fields, clinical photography, editorial serif typography, antique-gold accents, and integrated Tao brush-circle branding for Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, and Stories.

**Reference:** VISUAL_DOCTRINE_AUTHORITY.md (in this folder)

### Color Palette

- Charcoal black
- Ink navy
- Warm ivory (primary copy color)
- Restrained antique gold (emphasis, rules, selective words, brush-circle motif)
- Warm oak and natural skin tones (within clinical photography)

### Typography (Social Production)

**Primary Headlines:**
- Refined, high-contrast editorial serif
- Warm ivory color
- Strong, commanding presence
- For main message delivery

**Copy & Supporting Text:**
- Warm ivory on charcoal/navy backgrounds
- Generous leading and line spacing
- Mobile-legible sizing
- Clean, restrained treatment

**Brand Signature:**
- Small, widely tracked
- Warm ivory or antique gold
- Integrated into negative space (not a separate footer)

**Important:** Do NOT use Manrope sans-serif or chapter symbols in social production. These remain for institutional applications only.

### Photography Requirements

- Warm, dimensional natural light
- Cinematic low-key exposure
- Authentic manual-therapy or massage-therapy treatment-room context
- Adult clinician and patient at equal human scale
- Realistic posture, hands, expression, and skin tone
- Quiet clinical moments (not posed demonstrations)
- Clinician listening; patient speaking naturally
- No touch occurring in the scene
- No barriers (clipboard, laptop, phone)

### Brand Motif

**Tao Brush Circle:**
- Antique gold open brush circle
- Integrated into composition (not pasted on)
- Primary Tao motif for social production
- Paired with small brand signature when appropriate
- Secondary to the teaching message

**Important:** Do NOT use decorative Chinese characters. Do NOT use chapter symbols or ripple watermarks in social context.

### Composition & Gradients

- Charcoal-to-transparent or ink-navy-to-transparent gradient overlays where copy requires contrast
- Gradient blended naturally into photograph (not hard vertical division)
- Preserve faces, hands, meaningful body language
- Maintain warm highlights and natural skin tones
- Generous negative space for typography
- Branding emerges from composition (not pasted on)

### Structure

- Blend dark editorial fields and photography naturally
- Use thin antique-gold rules sparingly (hierarchy support only)
- Avoid rigid blocks unless content requires an editorial grid
- Avoid separate branded footer bars
- Avoid boxed white logo panels
- Keep copy mobile-legible
- All text inside mobile-safe margins
