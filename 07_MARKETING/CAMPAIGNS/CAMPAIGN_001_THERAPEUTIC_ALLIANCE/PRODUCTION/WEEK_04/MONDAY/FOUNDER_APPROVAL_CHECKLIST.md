## Governing Idea — Approve or Adjust

Listening is not the pause before the clinician speaks. It is part of the clinical process.

Before a clinician explains, corrects, tests, or touches, the patient is already communicating through words, pace, hesitation, posture, breath, and what they choose not to say. The clinician's task is not to extract a better story. It is to create enough room for the useful story to emerge.
