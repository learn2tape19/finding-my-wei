## Foundational Principles

Every Week 4 Monday social visual should feel like a page from a premium clinical campaign—cinematic, editorial, clinically grounded, warm but restrained, and emotionally present without being theatrical.

**Visual Language:**
- Charcoal black and ink-navy tonal fields
- Warm, dimensional clinical photography
- Editorial serif typography (refined, high-contrast)
- Warm ivory primary copy
- Restrained antique-gold emphasis
- Thin gold rules (used sparingly)
- Integrated open Tao brush circle
- Small, widely-tracked brand signature
- No footer bars; no boxed logo panels
- No chapter symbols; no ripple watermarks

---

## CANONICAL FIREFLY PROMPT

Use this foundation for all 6 assets. Customize per platform and composition, but maintain all core requirements:

```
Create a premium editorial social image for The Tao of Clinical Touch. 

Use cinematic charcoal and ink-navy tonal fields blended naturally with 
warm, dimensional clinical photography. 

Show an authentic manual-therapy or massage-therapy intake conversation 
with realistic adult subjects, natural posture, anatomically accurate hands, 
restrained expression, and warm treatment-room light. 

The clinician is listening with relaxed attention; the patient is speaking 
naturally. Both seated at equal eye level. A treatment table softly visible 
in the background. No touch occurring. No barriers (clipboard, laptop, phone).

Preserve generous negative space for editorial typography. 

The atmosphere should feel thoughtful, clinically grounded, sophisticated, 
and quietly human—like a premium clinical book campaign. 

Use a palette of charcoal black, ink navy, warm ivory, restrained antique gold, 
warm oak, and natural skin tones. 

Do not create text, logos, symbols, signage, footer bars, boxed panels, 
medical icons, glowing anatomy, stock smiles, theatrical emotion, or 
generic wellness imagery.
```

---

## NEGATIVE PROMPT

```
Corporate social template, bright blue healthcare graphic, solid footer bar, 
white logo box, pasted-on branding, stock-photo smile, exaggerated empathy, 
psychotherapy scene, hospital scene, white coat, stethoscope, clipboard barrier, 
glowing anatomy, sound waves, speech bubbles, floating icons, Chinese characters, 
text, watermark, distorted hands, extra fingers, plastic skin, harsh fluorescent 
light, oversaturated color, excessive blur, chapter symbols, ripple watermark, 
Manrope font, sage-led branding
```

# ASSET 1: INSTAGRAM FEED (1080 × 1350 px)

**Filename:** `Campaign_001_Week_04_Monday_Instagram_Feed_1080x1350_v1.jpg`  
**Format:** JPG (quality 85–90) or PNG  
**Target Size:** Under 300 KB  
**Platform:** Instagram Feed (Portrait)

## Firefly Generation

**Prompt Customization:**
Use canonical Firefly prompt. Add: "Portrait orientation (1080 × 1350). Preserve generous negative space in lower third for headline placement. Warm, dimensional intake conversation moment."

**Expected Output:**
- Clinical photography of intake conversation
- Patient speaking naturally; clinician listening with relaxed attention
- Warm natural light; treatment-room context
- No touch; no barriers
- Realistic, anatomically accurate
- Ready for gradient overlay and typography

## Adobe Express Build Process

### Step 1: Image Placement
- Import Firefly image
- Set canvas to 1080 × 1350 px
- Place image full bleed

### Step 2: Gradient Overlay
- Add charcoal-to-transparent or navy-to-transparent gradient
- Apply gradient to lower third (where headline copy will sit)
- Blend gradient smoothly into photograph
- DO NOT create hard vertical division
- Preserve faces, hands, meaningful body language
- Maintain warm highlights and natural skin tones

### Step 3: Headline Typography
- Text: `LISTENING IS A CLINICAL INTERVENTION.`
- Font: Approved editorial serif (refined, high-contrast)
- Color: Warm ivory (#f5f0e8 or similar)
- Size: Large, commanding (responsive to mobile legibility)
- Weight: Strong (bold or semi-bold)
- Placement: Lower third; centered or left-aligned
- Spacing: Generous leading; breathing room around text
- Mobile safe: Verify readable at small screen size

### Step 4: Optional Secondary Line
- Text: `Before you explain, make room for what matters.`
- Font: Same editorial serif
- Color: Warm ivory
- Size: Smaller than primary headline
- Placement: Below primary headline
- Only include if composition remains quiet and mobile-legible

### Step 5: Brand Circle Motif
- Add antique-gold open Tao brush circle
- Integrate into composition (not pasted on)
- Placement: Lower right, or near brand signature, or integrated with headline area
- Size: Proportional to overall composition (not dominant)
- Color: Restrained antique gold (#b8860b or similar muted tone)
- Opacity: 100% (fully opaque)

### Step 6: Brand Signature
- Text: Small, widely tracked `THE TAO OF CLINICAL TOUCH`
- Font: Editorial serif or approved brand font
- Color: Warm ivory or antique gold
- Size: Small (10–12pt equivalent)
- Placement: Lower right or integrated with negative space
- Important: Do NOT place in a separate footer bar

### Step 7: Optional Thin Gold Rule
- Use one thin antique-gold horizontal or accent rule
- Placement: Above or below headline, or accent to brush circle
- Only if it supports hierarchy and doesn't clutter
- Thickness: 1–2 px
- Color: Restrained antique gold

## Quality Control Checklist

- [ ] Image remains warm and cinematic (not cold corporate aesthetic)
- [ ] Gradient blends naturally (not a visible hard line)
- [ ] No faces or hands obscured by gradient or text
- [ ] Typography is warm ivory on dark background (high contrast)
- [ ] Serif font is refined and high-contrast (not corporate sans-serif)
- [ ] Antique gold is restrained and elegant (not bright or excessive)
- [ ] Brush circle is integrated (not pasted on as separate layer)
- [ ] Branding emerges from composition (not external to design)
- [ ] Text is readable at mobile size (minimum 18pt equivalent)
- [ ] No footer bar; no white boxed logo panel
- [ ] Overall design resembles premium clinical editorial (not healthcare template)
- [ ] File size under 300 KB
