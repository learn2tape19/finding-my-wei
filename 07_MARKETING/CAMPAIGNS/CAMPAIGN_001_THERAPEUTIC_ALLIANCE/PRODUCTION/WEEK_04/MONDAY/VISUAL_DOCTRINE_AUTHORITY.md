## Clarification: Intentional Channel-Specific Evolution

On July 9, 2026, the Tao Visual Identity System v1.0 was established as a canonical institutional standard. On July 27, 2026, the Canonical Social Visual Doctrine v1.0 was approved, creating an intentional channel-specific split—not an accidental contradiction.

**This is authorized and by design.**

---

## Authority Hierarchy for Social Visuals

**For all social and campaign applications, use this hierarchy:**

1. **Founder-Approved Canonical Social Visual Doctrine v1.0** — July 27, 2026 (AUTHORITATIVE)
2. **Campaign-specific approved visual benchmark** (e.g., Campaign 001 Week 4 Monday Editorial v3)
3. **Tao Visual Identity System v1.0** — July 9, 2026 (only where it does not conflict with #1 and #2)

---

## Social & Campaign Visuals (Doctrine v1.0 Authoritative)

**The July 27 Social Visual Doctrine governs:**

- Instagram and Facebook feed posts
- LinkedIn graphics
- Stories and Carousels
- Quote cards
- Article-promotion graphics
- Book-promotion graphics
- Campaign derivatives
- Firefly prompts
- Adobe Express production

**Visual Requirements:**
- Charcoal and ink-navy tonal fields (gradient overlays)
- Warm, dimensional clinical photography
- Editorial serif typography (refined, high-contrast)
- Warm ivory copy
- Restrained antique-gold emphasis
- Thin gold rules (used sparingly)
- Integrated open Tao brush circle motif
- Small, widely-tracked brand signature
- No footer bars; no boxed logo panels
- No chapter symbols; no ripple watermarks (in social context)

**Firefly Prompt Foundation:**
Use the canonical Adobe Firefly prompt from the Social Visual Doctrine, customized per week/day/message.

**Adobe Express Build Instructions:**
Follow the deterministic typography and branding instructions in the Social Visual Doctrine exactly.

## Institutional & Non-Social Applications (System v1.0 Remains)

**The July 9 Tao Visual Identity System v1.0 remains applicable to:**

- Internal documentation
- Educational diagrams
- Chapter-specific materials
- Long-form documents
- Website elements not functioning as campaign graphics
- Existing assets not scheduled for redesign
- Institutional reference materials
- Non-promotional educational content

**These applications retain:**
- Chapter symbols (03_eye.svg, etc.)
- Ripple watermark
- Manrope sans-serif typography
- Warm linen/oak/sage/cream palette
- Original institutional branding structure

**Important:** Do not carry Chapter symbols, ripple watermarks, or Manrope typography into social production unless a future assignment explicitly requires them and includes Founder approval for the exception.

---

## Why This Split Exists

**Social Visual Evolution (July 27):**
Campaign 001 Week 4 Monday required a more cinematic, dramatic visual presentation—one that uses charcoal/navy tonal fields, antique gold accents, and editorial serif typography to communicate sophistication and clinical authority at the social scale. This works for campaigns, Stories, and promotional graphics.

**Institutional Stability (July 9):**
The existing system remains authoritative for internal materials, documentation, and non-social applications where the warm, restrained aesthetic and chapter-symbol system serve their intended purpose.

**No Contradiction:**
These are channel-specific applications. A social Story uses charcoal/navy/gold. An internal educational diagram uses warm linen/oak/sage and chapter symbols. Both are correct in their context.
