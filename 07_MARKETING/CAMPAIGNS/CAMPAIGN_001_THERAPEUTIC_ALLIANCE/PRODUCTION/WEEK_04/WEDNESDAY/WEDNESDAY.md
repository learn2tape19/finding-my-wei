## Core Message Architecture (Approved by Handoff)

### Primary Anchor
**THE SYMPTOM IS ONLY THE FIRST SENTENCE.**

### Supporting Line
**What else needs to be said?**

### Governing Idea
A patient's opening complaint—where it hurts, what triggered it, how long it's lasted—is clinically useful information. It is rarely the complete story. When a clinician creates room for the person to continue (without rushing to explain, correct, or treat), additional information often emerges: what the symptom interrupts, what the patient fears it means, what matters most to them, or what they've already tried. This second layer of information changes what becomes available for assessment.

The practice is not to interpret silence or over-analyze pauses. It is to notice whether you are still listening—or whether you have already begun building your clinical answer.

### Desired Reader Response
"I hear this every day—the patient tells me one thing, then tells me something completely different if I just wait. I never connected that to my own listening."

## Visual System (Social Visual Doctrine v1.0)

### Canonical Approach

All Week 4 Wednesday social visuals use the Founder-approved Canonical Social Visual Doctrine v1.0 (July 27, 2026). This doctrine governs charcoal/navy tonal fields, clinical photography, editorial serif typography, antique-gold accents, and integrated Tao brush-circle branding for Instagram carousel, Facebook, LinkedIn, and Stories.

**Reference:** VISUAL_DOCTRINE_AUTHORITY.md (WEEK_04 root folder)

### Color Palette
- Charcoal black
- Ink navy
- Warm ivory (primary copy color)
- Restrained antique gold (emphasis, rules, selective words, brush-circle motif)
- Warm oak and natural skin tones (within clinical photography)

### Typography (Social Production)
- **Headlines:** Refined editorial serif, warm ivory, strong commanding presence
- **Copy:** Warm ivory on charcoal/navy backgrounds, generous leading
- **Brand Signature:** Small, widely tracked, integrated into negative space

**Important:** Do NOT use Manrope sans-serif or chapter symbols in social production.

### Photography & Composition
- Warm, dimensional natural light; cinematic low-key exposure
- Authentic manual-therapy treatment-room context
- Adult clinician and patient at equal human scale
- Realistic posture, hands, expression, and skin tone
- Quiet clinical moments (not posed demonstrations)
- Patient speaking or in moment of internal clarity; clinician attending
- No barriers (clipboard, laptop, phone); no touch occurring
- Generous negative space for editorial typography
- Charcoal-to-transparent gradient overlays where copy needs contrast
- Tao brush circle integrated into composition (not pasted on)
