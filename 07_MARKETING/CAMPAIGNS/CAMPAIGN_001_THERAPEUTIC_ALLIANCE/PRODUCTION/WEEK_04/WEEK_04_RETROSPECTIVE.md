## Week 4 Narrative Arc — The Teaching Progression

### What Week 4 Accomplished

Week 4 was designed as a coherent four-day teaching sequence, not a collection of unrelated posts. Each day advanced one clinical idea, building toward a complete framework for listening as clinical practice.

**The progression:**

1. **Monday — Listening as Intervention**
   - **Anchor:** "Listening is a clinical intervention"
   - **Question:** Is listening passive or active clinical work?
   - **Reader Response:** "I do this, but I may move to interpretation sooner than I realize"

2. **Wednesday — Listen Beyond the First Sentence**
   - **Anchor:** "The symptom is only the first sentence"
   - **Deepening:** What else needs to be said? What changes when you remain genuinely curious?
   - **Reader Response:** "I hear this every day—if I just wait, the patient says something completely different"

3. **Thursday — Confirm Before Explaining**
   - **Anchor:** "The timing of explanation"
   - **Core Message:** "Understanding begins with being understood"
   - **Teaching:** The patient needs to feel understood *before* your explanation lands
   - **Reader Response:** "I've offered the same explanation to different patients and gotten completely different responses. I think this is why"

4. **Friday — Let Listening Shape the Plan**
   - **Anchor:** "Let what you heard change what you do next"
   - **Supporting Line:** "Listening becomes clinical when it shapes the plan"
   - **Synthesis:** The patient's priorities, concerns, and expectations meaningfully influence sequence, focus, consent, and expectations
   - **Reader Response:** "When I actually build my plan around what I heard—not just what I saw or tested—the outcomes are different"

**Weekly synthesis:**
> **Listen → Remain curious → Confirm understanding → Let what was heard shape the plan**

This is not four separate thoughts. This is one continuous teaching arc that builds from observation to integrated clinical practice.

- **Facebook:** Slightly more narrative and conversational. Make the idea accessible. End with natural discussion prompt.
- **LinkedIn:** Professional and practice-oriented. Emphasize operational consequences. Address clinicians and educators.

**Evidence:**

- LinkedIn emphasized professional application and institutional learning

**Carry Forward for Week 5:**

- Instagram should remain concise; Facebook can be more conversational; LinkedIn should be clinically precise and practice-focused
- One useful question is stronger than multiple generic engagement prompts

---

### D. Scientific Language Restraint

**Achievement:** Content preserved credibility by avoiding universal causal claims and unsupported neuroscience.

**Language Discipline:**
- Use "may," "can," "often," and "is more likely to" where appropriate
- Do not promise safety, trust, regulation, healing, or improved outcomes
- Do not use diagnostic language
- Do not invent statistics, quotations, citations, or mechanisms

**Evidence:**
- No claims about neuroplasticity, mirror neurons, or vagal regulation
- No universal assertions ("Listening always...", "The patient will...")
- No attribution of healing or transformation to listening alone

**Carry Forward for Week 5:**
- Maintain scientific restraint as a non-negotiable standard
- When describing clinical effects, use hedging language appropriately
- Do not oversell the mechanism or outcome of listening

### F. Completed-Asset Discipline

**Achievement:** Visuals were created, reviewed, and Founder-approved before final repository staging. Once approved, assets were not regenerated, re-edited, or sent back through design tools.

**Process That Worked:**
1. Founder-approved Editorial v3 exports received
2. Checksums verified against canonical source
3. Production documentation staged around approved assets (not vice versa)
4. No Firefly or Adobe Express regeneration after approval

**What Did NOT Happen:**
- Approved assets were not sent back through Firefly
- Approved assets were not re-edited in Adobe Express
- No approved JPG was replaced with a different version
- No conversion from JPG to PNG or other format occurred
- No recompression or alteration was attempted

**Carry Forward for Week 5:**
- Once visuals are Founder-approved, production documentation must surround them, not restart them
- Firefly and Adobe Express instructions are future-production reference, not active rebuild prompts
- Approved assets are immutable during staging and repository work
- Never regenerate a Founder-approved export

### G. Canonical Filename Discipline

**Achievement:** Creative iteration labels (Editorial v2, v3) describe the development process. The approved production export is promoted to canonical `v1` filename without renaming, conversion, or recompression.

**Naming Convention That Worked:**
- Creative iteration: "Editorial v3" describes the design refinement process
- Canonical production export: `v1` describes the approved production artifact
- Repository staging: Exact `v1` filename, no browser suffixes like `(1)` or `(2)`

**What Did NOT Happen:**
- No approved file was renamed with browser suffixes
- No file was converted from one format to another during naming
- No file size changed due to renaming

**Carry Forward for Week 5:**
- Creative labels are internal process documentation
- Approved production exports are always `v1` in the repository
- Renaming must never involve format conversion, cropping, or recompression
- Browser-added suffixes like `(1)` and `(2)` are never canonical filenames

## Problems Week 5 Must Not Repeat

### Preventable Production Failures (Checklist Items)

1. ❌ **Confusing institutional identity with social visual doctrine**
   - Institutional system (July 9 Tao Visual Identity v1.0) ≠ Social system (July 27 Social Visual Doctrine v1.0)
   - Use correct doctrine per channel
   - Pre-checklist review: Which doctrine applies to this asset?

2. ❌ **Regenerating Founder-approved assets through Firefly or Adobe Express**
   - Once approved, assets are immutable
   - Production documentation goes *around* assets, not *through* them
   - Pre-checklist review: Are these Founder-approved? If yes, do not regenerate.

3. ❌ **Treating Editorial v3 as incorrect because final files use canonical `v1` names**
   - Editorial v3 describes the approved design iteration
   - `v1` describes the canonical production export
   - Pre-checklist review: Is this a naming issue or a production issue?

4. ❌ **Accepting browser suffixes like `(1)` or `(2)` as production filenames**
   - Browser-added suffixes are never canonical
   - Rename to exact spec before staging
   - Pre-checklist review: Does this filename match the handoff spec exactly?

5. ❌ **Replacing approved JPEG with larger or visually different PNG**
   - Format changes indicate regeneration
   - File size increases indicate recompression
   - Pre-checklist review: Is byte size exactly what was approved?

6. ❌ **Claiming checksum verification without comparing source and staged files**
   - Verification requires actual SHA-256 comparison
   - Verbal confirmation is not the same as byte-for-byte match
   - Pre-checklist review: Did I actually run `shasum -a 256` on both files?

7. ❌ **Publishing to wrong calendar date**
   - Monday July 29 is Wednesday, not Monday
   - Verify day of week before scheduling
   - Pre-checklist review: Is the date correct? Is the day of week correct?

8. ❌ **Backdating or silently rescheduling delayed posts**
   - Record actual scheduled time, not intended time
   - Controlled delays are documented, not hidden
   - Pre-checklist review: Is this a controlled delay? Is it recorded?

9. ❌ **Describing assets as awaiting design after Founder approval**
   - Approved visuals are not in production limbo
   - Use status: "Founder-approved visuals received" not "Visuals to be created"
   - Pre-checklist review: Does the status language match the actual state?

10. ❌ **Marking scheduled content Published before verifying live**
    - Use "Scheduled" when confirmed in platform scheduler
    - Use "Published" only after verified live on platform
    - Pre-checklist review: Have I verified this live? Or only scheduled?

11. ❌ **Reversing Story sequence in documentation**
    - Frame order is critical to narrative
    - Document and publish in approved order only
    - Pre-checklist review: Is Story order Frame 01 → 02 → 03?

12. ❌ **Presenting shortened, summarized, or AI-paraphrased endorsement as direct quote**
    - Quotation marks require source verification
    - Paraphrases must be clearly labeled
    - Pre-checklist review: Can I verify this is a direct quote? If not, remove quotes.

13. ❌ **Allowing repetitive AI cadence or ornamental language to overpower Founder's voice**
    - Excessive three-part constructions
    - Repetitive reflection-question patterns
    - Ornamental cadence that doesn't sound like clinical observation
    - Pre-checklist review: Does this sound like Drew? Or like AI templating?
