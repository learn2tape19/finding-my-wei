# Campaign 001 — Current Authority Register

**Effective:** August 3, 2026 (asset-persistence and completeness rules added August 21, 2026)

**Purpose:** Identify the current rule when historical records conflict

**Authority:** Founder-approved decisions and verified campaign closeout evidence

---

## Authority Order

When two records conflict, use this order:

1. Explicit Founder approval or correction.
2. Approved final asset and its manifest.
3. This current authority register.
4. Current campaign architecture and publishing standards.
5. Historical briefs, prompts, drafts, and proposed structures.

Historical files are preserved as evidence. They do not automatically remain current doctrine.

4. Update the publication ledger and affected manifest.
