## Independent composition and export method

Used the built-in image-generation tool in three separate calls with separate Story canvases and distinct scene/composition instructions. The approved Tuesday Feed served only as a style reference. None of these Stories is a resized Feed, Landscape, or another Story.

The generator returned each independently composed Story at **941×1672**, despite requests for native 1080×1920. Before delivery, each same-format Story composition was proportionally fitted to **855×1520**, then centered inside its exact **1080×1920** cream canvas. This provides 200px outer top/bottom margins and approximately 112px side margins, in addition to the artwork's internal spacing. No axis stretching, distortion, content cropping, or cross-format repurposing occurred. Final-size exports were reopened and proofread after this operation.

Exact historical font files and color tokens were not supplied; visual matching is against the founder-approved Tuesday artwork.
