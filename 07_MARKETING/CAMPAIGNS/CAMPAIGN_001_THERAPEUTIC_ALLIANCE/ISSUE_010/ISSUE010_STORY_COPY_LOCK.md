# ISSUE 010 — LOCKED STORY COPY

**Authority:** Story copy derives from `ISSUE010_MASTER_COPY.md`. Each day's three Stories advance that day's canonical concept and no other. Once artwork is built, the text rendered inside the approved Story artwork becomes authoritative for Story publishing — and must match this document exactly.

**Story architecture (every day):**
- **Story 1 — IDEA** — states the day's concept plainly.
- **Story 2 — TENSION** — names the pressure, habit, or cost that works against it.
- **Story 3 — APPLICATION** — turns it into something the clinician can do, and carries the CTA.

**Closing line:** every Story 3 ends with `READ TODAY'S POST.` Stories 1 and 2 do not carry a CTA.

**Recurring brand line:** `Possibility is what becomes available when we stop needing to be right.` Use on Story 3 only, above the CTA. Do not place it on every frame.
