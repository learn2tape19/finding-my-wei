## 1. Canonical visual doctrine

Source of authority: `ADOBE_BRAND_MANIFEST.md`. This is the permanent Tao editorial system — **not** the Issue 009 midweek replacement aesthetic (stepping stones, reeds, warm amber gradient), which was an approved emergency repair and carries no doctrinal weight.

**Palette**
| Role | Color | Hex |
|---|---|---|
| Primary text, headers, identity | Deep Clinical Navy | `#182633` |
| Accent, dividers, highlights | Antique Gold | `#B8860B` |
| Secondary/supporting text | Charcoal | `#3A3A3A` |
| Background, body text on navy | Warm Ivory | `#F7F4EE` |

**Typography**
- **Cormorant Garamond** — publication wordmark, editorial headlines, primary messaging (Regular / Medium / Bold).
- **Source Sans Pro** — issue numbers, labels, metadata, supporting text (Regular / SemiBold / Bold).

**Fixed masthead block (every asset, top):**
Gold enso brush circle → `THE TAO OF CLINICAL TOUCH` → gold rule → `THE NEUROSCIENCE OF PERMISSION`.

**Fixed footer block (every asset, bottom):**
Navy band with a gold wave edge and enso mark, reading `ISSUE 010 POSSIBILITY` · `{DAY}` · `WEEK 10`.

**Recurring brand element — the still surface.** Issue 010 carries forward the restrained water motif on an ivory-to-navy gradient. Per the Issue 009 ripple rule: **restrained, never an escalation device.** Do not add or intensify droplets, splashes, or ripple layers across a day's Story sequence, and do not introduce a new motif midweek.

**Issue 010 motif direction — the open surface.** Where Issue 009 used the ripple (something arriving), Issue 010 uses **open, unbroken water with visible depth and light** — quiet, wide, uncommitted. Possibility is space that has not yet been filled. One motif for all 20 assets, varying only in framing and light, never in kind.

**Hierarchy contract (all 20 assets):**
1. Masthead block
2. **Primary headline** — Cormorant Garamond, navy, largest type on the canvas
3. **Gold accent clause or rule** — the one gold typographic element
4. **Supporting thought** — Charcoal/navy, clearly subordinate
5. **Closing thought / CTA** — smallest body tier
6. Footer block

Exactly **one** gold typographic emphasis per asset. Never two competing gold moments.

**Feed vs Story 1 differentiation — required.** By architecture, Story 1 (IDEA) states the same concept as the Feed headline. The two must not be near-identical images. Rule: **Feed is horizon-framed and composed** (wider negative space, headline centred in the upper third); **Story 1 is close-framed and vertical** (tighter crop on the water surface, headline dominant). Different supporting lines are already specified below.

**Accessibility:** every asset requires objective alt text written from the rendered artwork at staging time. Do not pre-write alt text from this brief.

## 2. FEED ASSETS — 5 × 1080×1350

Feed copy is authoritative from `ISSUE010_MASTER_COPY.md`. Composition: horizon-framed, generous negative space above the water line, headline in the upper third, footer band across the base.

### MON — `ISSUE010_MON_FEED_1080x1350.png`
- **Headline:** POSSIBILITY BEGINS WITH NOT KNOWING
- **Support:** An encounter that can only confirm what we believe cannot teach us anything.
- **Close:** Not knowing is not a gap. It is an opening.
- **Gold emphasis:** `NOT KNOWING` in the headline.
- **Visual concept:** Widest, emptiest frame of the week. Open water, high horizon, ivory sky occupying most of the canvas. Establishes the week's negative space as a visual argument.

### TUE — `ISSUE010_TUE_FEED_1080x1350.png`
- **Headline:** MORE THAN ONE THING CAN BE TRUE
- **Support:** Irritable tissue. A depleted person. Movement that is still improving.
- **Close:** Holding several truths isn't indecision. It's accuracy.
- **Gold emphasis:** `ONE THING` in the headline.
- **Visual concept:** Two distinct light sources on the surface — a reflected sky and a lower ambient glow — coexisting without merging. Multiplicity rendered as light, not as division. No literal split-screen.

### WED — `ISSUE010_WED_FEED_1080x1350.png`
- **Headline:** LEAVE ROOM FOR WHAT YOU DIDN'T EXPECT
- **Support:** A plan should organize the session, not seal it.
- **Close:** If every minute is assigned, nothing new can land.
- **Gold emphasis:** `LEAVE ROOM` in the headline.
- **Visual concept:** Deliberate unfilled area — a wide band of empty water carrying no type, roughly the lower third. The composition demonstrates the argument: room left on purpose.

### THU — `ISSUE010_THU_FEED_1080x1350.png`
- **Headline:** THE OUTCOME MAY NOT LOOK LIKE THE PLAN
- **Support:** Sometimes what arrives is an easier breath, not more range.
- **Close:** Measure what happened, not only what you predicted.
- **Gold emphasis:** `MAY NOT` in the headline.
- **Visual concept:** Light falling off-centre — the brightest point sits away from where the composition's symmetry predicts. Subtle, not disorienting.

### FRI — `ISSUE010_FRI_FEED_1080x1350.png`
- **Headline:** POSSIBILITY IS THE PRACTICE
- **Support:** Not optimism. Not temperament. A set of choices, repeated.
- **Close:** Timing opens possibility.
- **Gold emphasis:** `THE PRACTICE` in the headline.
- **Visual concept:** The week's most composed and settled frame — the calmest water, the most resolved light. Reads as arrival. Closes the arc that began in Issue 009.

## 4. Production rules
- Build every asset at the source resolution the generator produces, then normalize to exact production pixels for `APPROVED_ASSETS/`. Retain the unmodified source in `SOURCE_APPROVED/` under `{DAY}_{SLOT}__{delivered-filename}.png`.
- Feed artwork is built from **Feed** copy in `ISSUE010_MASTER_COPY.md`. Story artwork is built from `ISSUE010_STORY_COPY_LOCK.md`. Never cross them.
- No hashtags, stickers, links, or CTAs on Feed artwork — captions carry those.
- Verify each rendered asset visually against its locked copy before hashing.
- One gold typographic emphasis per asset. One motif for the issue. No new visual doctrine introduced mid-issue.
